Hotkeys, Blender style

  • G move   R rotate   S scale
  • then X Y Z locks an axis (in the Global or Local orientation set in the header), press it again for the other one; Shift+axis locks a plane
  • type a number for exact values; Ctrl held flips snapping; Shift+Tab turns snapping on or off; V held snaps to points
  • click or Enter confirms, right click or Esc cancels
  • click selects, Shift+click adds to the selection, drag for box select
  • A select all, Alt+A none
  • Shift+A add, Shift+D duplicate, X delete, H hide, Alt+H show all
  • I key the selection at the playhead; double-click a key to go to its frame and edit it
  • Tab edit the shape of a collider; 1 points 2 edges 3 faces, E extrude
  • Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Shift+Z redo
  • F3 search the settings; over a setting: Backspace resets it, Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V copy and paste, right-click for more; drag a value sideways to change it, click it to type
  • orbit: middle mouse, right drag or Alt+drag; pan: Shift+middle; zoom: wheel or Ctrl+Alt+drag
  • F or numpad . focus the selection, Home frame all; numpad 1 3 7 front, side, top; numpad 5 orthographic
  • drag the thin bars between the panels to resize them
